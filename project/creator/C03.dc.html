Spec notes · C03MVP
Mobile · 390 px · no tab bar (onboarding)
Purpose. Confirm who the seller is and where to pay them, using provider-hosted checks.
Roles. Creator / LIVE Seller.
Rules
  • Identity (photo ID and selfie) runs with an identity provider; Vyralink stores only the result.
  • Payout account is set up on the payment provider’s page; Vyralink stores a token and last 4 digits.
  • Tax form (W-9 in the US, W-8BEN elsewhere) collected by the payment provider where required.
  • Browsing and applying are allowed before verification; accepting a paid booking is not.
States and errors
  • Identity failed (name mismatch) → retry or support
  • Bank account could not be verified → add another
  • Tax form missing → payouts held, jobs still allowed
