Spec notes · O04MVP
Purpose. The booths a location can sell, with their kit and status.
Roles. Location Manager.
Rules
  • Booth type limits which campaign categories it can host (e.g. Beauty, Fashion, Electronics, Flexible).
  • Maintenance blocks remove the booth from availability (O05).
  • A booth can’t be deleted with future bookings; set it to Retired after they end.
States and errors
  • Booth in maintenance with bookings → reassign prompt
  • Kit item missing → booth flagged “Not ready”
