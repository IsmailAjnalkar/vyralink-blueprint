Spec notes · F02MVP
Purpose. Bill brands for Creator Hub services and track what is owed.
Roles. Finance Administrator.
Rules
  • Invoice statuses: Draft → Issued → Paid; or Overdue, Disputed (amount paused), Void, Credited.
  • Every line has a basis (fixed price, hours × rate, % × eligible attributable GMV).
  • Performance-fee invoices are created as Drafts and issued only after results lock (F05).
  • Collection, card storage and reminders run through the payment provider; Vyralink stores tokens and statuses.
  • Prices come from the price book; custom prices need approval.
States and errors
  • Payment failed → provider retries; grace period then bookings flagged (O24)
  • Disputed line → paused, linked to A08
  • Refund after invoicing → credit note
Goes to. F05 · B16 · O24 · A08
