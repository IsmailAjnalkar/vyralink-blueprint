Spec notes · A07MVP
Purpose. Platform view of money moving through the payment provider.
Roles. Platform Super Administrator; Finance Administrator read-only.
Rules
  • Vyralink holds no customer funds and runs no escrow. Brand charges and seller payouts are separate provider-managed flows.
  • Customer purchases happen in TikTok Shop and never appear here.
  • Vyralink subscription revenue and Creator Hub service revenue are reported separately; the arrangement between the two companies is [TO DEFINE].
  • Webhook failures are retried; nothing is marked paid without the provider’s confirmation.
States and errors
  • Webhook backlog → statuses delayed banner
  • Provider outage → charges and payouts paused
