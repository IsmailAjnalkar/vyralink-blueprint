Spec notes · A10MVP
Purpose. Account protection policies, security events and privacy requests.
Roles. Platform Super Administrator.
Rules
  • Two-step verification required for: Super Admins, Creator Hub staff, Brand Owners (configurable for others).
  • Admins sign in with single sign-on only.
  • Lockout after 5 failed sign-ins in 10 minutes.
  • Privacy requests (export, delete) tracked to completion; deletion respects legal retention of financial records.
States and errors
  • Suspicious sign-in → user notified, session challenged
  • Deletion blocked by open bookings or unpaid earnings → reason shown
