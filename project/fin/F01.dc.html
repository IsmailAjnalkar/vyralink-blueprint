Spec notes · F01MVP
Purpose. Creator Hub’s money position and the finance work queue.
Roles. Finance Administrator; Location Managers see their location’s summary.
Rules
  • Revenue = Creator Hub service fees (production, studio, performance, packages). GMV is brand sales and never counted as revenue.
  • Seller compensation is an expense, accrued when earned and paid in payout runs.
  • Vyralink holds no customer funds and no escrow: brand charges and seller payouts are separate flows through the payment provider.
  • Recognition (as configured with your accountant): production fee when the session is delivered; performance fee when results are locked.
States and errors
  • Provider sync delayed → figures show last sync time
  • Period not closed → “Provisional” label
