Spec notes · B18MVP
Purpose. Members, roles, channels, security and notification settings for one organization.
Roles. Brand Owner (Marketing Manager: own notification settings only).
Rules
  • Permissions are enforced on the server per organization and role; hidden buttons are not the control.
  • At least one Brand Owner must remain.
  • Role changes and removals are audit-logged (A09).
  • Invites expire after 7 days.
States and errors
  • Last owner → blocked with explanation
  • No permission → 403 with Request access
  • Invite bounced → Resend or edit email
