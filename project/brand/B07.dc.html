Spec notes · B07MVP
Purpose. Define what is sold, when, by what kind of seller, at which studio, and on what money terms.
Roles. Brand Marketing Manager creates; Brand Owner approves over threshold.
Rules
  • Saves a Draft after every step.
  • Only Approved products can be selected.
  • Publishing needs a Verified brand (B02) and approval when budget exceeds the threshold.
  • Money terms are copied into the contract (B14, C12) when a seller is booked; later edits need both parties to re-sign.
  • Every calculation shows its basis: rate × hours, % × eligible attributable sales.
States and errors
  • No studio capacity → suggest slots or locations
  • Inventory below expected units → warning
  • Unverified brand → Publish disabled with link to B02
