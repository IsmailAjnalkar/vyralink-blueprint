Spec notes · O06MVP
Purpose. Every booking from hold to completion, with the actions each status allows.
Roles. Location Manager.
Rules
  • Booking statuses: Hold → Confirmed → Checked in → In progress → Completed; or Cancelled, No-show, Expired (hold).
  • Confirmed needs: contract signed by both, verified brand, booth free, producer assignable.
  • Rescheduling keeps the booking ID and history; brand and seller are notified.
  • Cancellations apply the signed policy and record who cancelled.
States and errors
  • Hold expiring → reminder to brand and manager
  • Producer not assignable → can’t confirm
  • Seller cancels → re-match request to brand (B12)
