Spec notes · B16MVP
Purpose. Fees the brand owes Vyralink / Creator Hub, with the basis of every line.
Roles. Brand Owner (Marketing Manager has no access by default; see B18).
Rules
  • Cards are stored by the payment provider; Vyralink stores only a token and last 4 digits.
  • Performance fee is invoiced after the returns window; later refunds create credit notes.
  • GMV never appears as an amount owed.
  • Every invoice line names its basis.
States and errors
  • Payment failed → retry and grace period
  • Dispute → amount paused
  • No access → 403 with Request access
