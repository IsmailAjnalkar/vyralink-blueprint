Spec notes · O05MVP
Purpose. Day view of every booth with bookings, holds, buffers and maintenance.
Roles. Location Manager; producers read-only.
Rules
  • Buffers (setup 30 min, teardown 15 min) are part of each booking and block the booth.
  • Server-side exclusion constraint: no two bookings or holds may overlap on the same booth, including buffers.
  • Holds expire after the hold window unless confirmed.
  • Drag to move needs a reason and notifies brand and seller.
States and errors
  • Overlap attempt → refused with the conflicting booking named
  • Hold expiring → highlighted
  • Studio closed → greyed
