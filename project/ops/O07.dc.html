Spec notes · O07MVP
Purpose. Who produces which session, and gaps before they become problems.
Roles. Location Manager; producers see their own shifts.
Rules
  • A producer can run one session at a time (setup to teardown).
  • Assigning checks shift, time off and certifications (e.g. electronics demos).
  • Unassigned confirmed bookings show as gaps 72 h ahead.
  • Producer reassignment notifies the seller and logs a reason.
States and errors
  • Producer calls in sick → affected sessions listed with suggested replacements
  • No replacement → escalate to manager; brand notified if start may move
