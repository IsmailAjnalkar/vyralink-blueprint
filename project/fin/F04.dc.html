Spec notes · F04MVP PHASE 2
Purpose. Pay sellers for locked earnings, through the payment provider.
Roles. Finance Administrator; second approver for large runs.
Rules
  • Runs include Locked lines only; held lines show why.
  • Runs over $[N] need two approvals.
  • The payment provider moves the money; Vyralink records the provider’s status per payout.
  • Holds: payout account unverified, tax form missing, dispute.
  • Year-end tax forms are issued by the provider where required.
  • Instant payouts and multiple schedules are Phase 2.
States and errors
  • Payout failed → seller fixes account (C22), retry in next run or manually
  • Provider outage → run paused, nothing double-paid (idempotency key per payout)
