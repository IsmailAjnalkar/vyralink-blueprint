Spec notes · O09MVP
Purpose. Log what brands send, check it against what’s expected, and store it.
Roles. Inventory Coordinator.
Rules
  • Brand registers shipments from the campaign (expected units, tracking).
  • Receiving records count, condition, photo and shelf.
  • Cutoff: samples must arrive [48] hours before the LIVE (configurable).
  • Shortfall at cutoff notifies brand and Location Manager.
States and errors
  • Missing at cutoff → reschedule or cancel by policy (B14)
  • Damaged → photo, brand asked to resend
  • Unexpected item → quarantine shelf
Goes to. O10 · O11 · B14
