Spec notes · B15MVP
Purpose. Show campaign results honestly, with their source and limits.
Roles. Brand Owner, Brand Marketing Manager.
Rules
  • Every metric shows source and last-updated time.
  • Metrics the source doesn’t provide show “Unavailable”, never zero or an estimate.
  • GMV / hour = GMV ÷ LIVE hours from the studio log.
  • Exports include the source column.
States and errors
  • Delayed → Pending with expected time
  • Attribution dispute → data review
  • No sessions in range → empty state
