Spec notes · A08MVP
Purpose. Resolve disagreements about money, results and deliverables, fairly and on record.
Roles. Platform Super Administrator (decides); Finance Administrator (applies outcomes).
Rules
  • Types: invoice, attribution / results, commission, deliverables, cancellation fee.
  • Opening a dispute holds only the disputed amount.
  • Both sides can add evidence for 5 days; the decision cites evidence.
  • Outcomes become credit notes (F02) or ledger adjustments (F03); appeal once within 7 days.
States and errors
  • No response from a party → decide on available evidence
  • Over SLA → escalated
