Spec notes · A05MVP
Purpose. Keep products, campaigns, reviews and messages within the rules.
Roles. Platform moderators (Super Admin or delegated).
Rules
  • Queues: product reviews (claims), campaign flags, reported reviews, flagged messages.
  • Restricted categories and claims list are managed in settings (A12).
  • Decisions: Approve, Request changes (reason), Remove; all logged and shown to the owner.
  • Automatic flags (e.g. contact details in messages, medical words in claims) are suggestions; a person decides.
States and errors
  • Change requested → owner sees reason in B05 / B07
  • Repeat violations → organization warning, then suspension (A02)
