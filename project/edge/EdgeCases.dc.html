Rules that apply to every error state

  • Every error names what happened and offers a next step; no dead ends.
  • Money is never silently changed: holds, adjustments and credit notes carry a reason and are logged.
  • Missing data is shown as Delayed or Unavailable, never as zero.
  • Permissions are checked on the server; the 403 screen offers Request access.
