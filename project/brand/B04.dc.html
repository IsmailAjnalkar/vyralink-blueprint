Spec notes · B04MVP
Purpose. One glance at campaigns, upcoming LIVEs, results and what needs action.
Roles. Brand Owner, Brand Marketing Manager (spend hidden if billing permission is off).
Rules
  • Attributable GMV = merchandise sales TikTok Shop attributes to this brand’s LIVE sessions. It is the brand’s sales, never Vyralink or Creator Hub revenue.
  • Every metric shows its source: Live, Delayed, Manual, Imported, Verified or Unavailable.
  • Campaign spend = Vyralink / Creator Hub fees only. Seller pay is Creator Hub’s cost and is not added again.
  • GMV ÷ spend is shown as a ratio and never labelled profit or ROI.
States and errors
  • No data yet → “Results appear after your first LIVE”
  • Import delayed → Delayed tag with timestamp
  • Unverified → publish actions disabled with reason
