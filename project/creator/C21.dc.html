Purpose. Show exactly how a session’s pay is calculated.
Roles. Creator / LIVE Seller.
Rules
  • Every line names its basis and source.
  • Commission = 5% × eligible attributable sales; eligible = GMV − refunds, cancellations and excluded orders.
  • Figures update as refunds arrive and lock when the returns window closes.
  • The brand’s performance fee uses the same eligible base.
  • A dispute holds only the disputed amount.
States and errors
  • Refund after lock → adjustment on the next payout
  • Dispute open → On hold
  • Results corrected → change shown with reason
