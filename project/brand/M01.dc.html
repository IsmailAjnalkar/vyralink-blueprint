Spec notes · M01PHASE 3
Purpose. Explainable seller suggestions for a campaign, once enough verified data exists.
Roles. Brand Owner, Brand Marketing Manager.
Rules
  • The score ranks sellers for this campaign. It is not a prediction of sales and is not scientifically validated until enough verified data exists.
  • Every factor shows its value, weight and source (Verified or Self-reported).
  • Sellers without history get “Limited data” instead of a score, and a fallback explanation.
  • Manual override (pin, hide) is always available and logged.
  • Behind feature flag ai_matching (A11).
States and errors
  • Flag off → B09 manual filters only
  • Data completeness low → score hidden
  • Seller becomes unavailable → removed from suggestions
