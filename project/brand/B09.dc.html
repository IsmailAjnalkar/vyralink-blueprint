Spec notes · B09MVP
Purpose. Find and shortlist LIVE sellers with manual filters (AI matching is Phase 3, see M01).
Roles. Brand Owner, Brand Marketing Manager.
Rules
  • Verified = computed from session data confirmed on Vyralink. Self-reported is labelled and never mixed into verified sorts.
  • New sellers appear with Academy results and a “New seller” label, not hidden.
  • Sellers can hide rate expectations (“On request”).
  • Invite requires a Published campaign; otherwise “Save to shortlist”.
States and errors
  • No results → widen filters or request sourcing
  • Seller busy on campaign date → warning on Invite
  • Unverified brand → Invite disabled
