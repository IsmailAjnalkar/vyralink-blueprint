Spec notes · B05MVP
Purpose. Keep every product a brand may sell in LIVE, with stock, samples and default commission.
Roles. Brand Owner, Brand Marketing Manager.
Rules
  • Statuses: Draft → Pending review → Approved or Rejected; Approved → Archived.
  • Only Approved products can be added to a campaign (B07).
  • SKU unique per brand; price > 0; inventory ≥ 0.
  • Commission here is the default; a campaign can override it.
  • Archive is blocked while the product is in a Scheduled or In-progress campaign.
States and errors
  • Rejected → reviewer note and Edit
  • Inventory 0 → warning when chosen for a campaign
  • CSV errors → row-level report
