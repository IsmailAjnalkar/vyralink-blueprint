Spec notes · A04MVP
Purpose. Decide on brand verifications and review seller identity results that need a human.
Roles. Platform verifier (Super Admin or delegated reviewer).
Rules
  • Brand documents are reviewed here; seller identity is checked by the identity provider and only exceptions come here.
  • Decision options: Approve, Request more information, Reject (reason from template + note).
  • SLA timer: [REVIEW SLA] business days from submission.
  • Reviewer can’t verify an organization they belong to.
  • Every decision is versioned and audit-logged.
States and errors
  • Document unreadable → Request more information
  • Expiring document → re-verification task
  • Identity provider degraded → seller checks queue here
