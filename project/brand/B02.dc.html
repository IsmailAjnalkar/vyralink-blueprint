Spec notes · B02MVP
Purpose. Prove the brand is a real business before it can publish campaigns, book booths or be invoiced.
Roles. Brand Owner submits; platform verifier decides (Super Admin A04).
Rules
  • Until Verified: products and campaigns can be drafted, but not published, booked or used to invite sellers.
  • Documents are encrypted at rest and visible only to the Brand Owner and platform verifiers.
  • Review target: [REVIEW SLA] business days (set by operations).
  • Each submission is versioned; decision and reviewer note are audit-logged.
  • Statuses: Not submitted → Submitted → In review → Verified, or Rejected → Resubmitted → In review.
States and errors
  • Rejected → reason shown, fields kept, resubmit
  • More information requested → the field is highlighted
  • Upload too large or wrong type → inline error
  • Document expires → re-verification request
