Spec notes · B01MVP
Purpose. Create a brand organization and its first user.
Roles. Visitor → Brand Owner.
Rules
  • Email and password, or a supported social sign-in (Google in MVP).
  • Creates the organization (status: Unverified) and the user with role Brand Owner.
  • Email must be verified before verification documents can be submitted (B02).
  • Password 12+ characters, checked against a breached-password list.
  • Sign-up attempts are rate-limited per IP (limit configurable).
States and errors
  • Duplicate email → sign in or reset password
  • Unverified email → banner on every page with Resend
  • Social sign-in cancelled → back here with a message
