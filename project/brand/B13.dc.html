Spec notes · B13MVP
Purpose. See every LIVE by seller, location and booth, and fix conflicts before they happen.
Roles. Brand Owner, Brand Marketing Manager (own campaigns only).
Rules
  • Studio holds expire after [HOLD WINDOW] hours unless confirmed (configurable).
  • The server refuses overlapping bookings for the same booth, producer or seller, including setup and teardown buffers.
  • Times show in the studio’s time zone; the viewer’s zone appears on hover and focus.
  • Brands see only their own sessions; booth availability shows as free or busy, never other brands’ names.
States and errors
  • Conflict → alternatives offered
  • Hold expiring in 2 h → reminder
  • Studio closed day → greyed out
